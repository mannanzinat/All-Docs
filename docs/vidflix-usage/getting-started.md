# Introduction

Welcome to Vidflix! This guide will walk you through the process of setting up and using your own personal streaming platform.

## What is Vidflix?

Vidflix is a self-hosted streaming platform that allows you to organize and stream your personal video library. It's like having your own private Netflix!

## Getting Started

This documentation is divided into the following sections:

*   **Installation:** Learn how to install and configure Vidflix on your server.
*   **Adding Content:** Discover how to add your movies and TV shows to the library.
*   **Customization:** Customize the look and feel of your Vidflix instance.
*   **User Management:** Manage users and their access to the platform.

If you have any questions or run into any issues, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
